How to play

  1. Read the numbers. Each number counts touching mines, including diagonals. A zero opens safe neighbors automatically.
  2. Reveal on your turn. Start anywhere with a safe opening of at least 2 × 2. Earn a first-clear bonus and points for its cascades. Later clears reward safe deductions and survived risks.
  3. The host chooses your lives. Choose 1–10 starting lives or Unlimited. A mine costs one life. When one player remains alive in a multiplayer round, they win that round. The overall winner has the most points.
  4. Flag at any time. Living players can flag while waiting or preparing. Press F to swap left/right Reveal and Flag. Press V to swap private/shared flags. Private is the default; hold a tile on touch screens to flag. Choose Shared for everyone to see, or Private for your own notes. You remove only your own flags. Flags are hints and don’t block reveals.
  5. Watch the timer. The host chooses 10–60 seconds or no timer. A timeout skips your turn for zero points. The preparation countdown runs before the first turn; mines stay hidden. Board-changing items unlock after the first clear.

Use Invite to share your room. Rejoin with your name to recover your seat. Late arrivals spectate until Play Again. Draft up to 3 new items before every round. Keep a unique collection for the game; eligible cards refresh every round, while opened Egg and used Echo Shard stay disabled. You have 2 rerolls for the entire draft. Passive items work automatically. The top queue shows who plays next; the divider marks the next cycle. Delayed or early players still take their turn. Cards explain when they can be used.

Guess points are rounded to a whole number; a forced 50/50 earns 40. Safe-clear underdog bonuses cap at ×1.25; risky or disruptive awards cap at ×3, with a total award cap of ×8. Large frontiers use an approximate risk, labeled “Approx.” Private flags are notes and earn no points. Each player can place shared flags up to the remaining mine count. Deductions ignore every flag.

Ghosts & balance

Eliminated participants get one harmless Ghost Flag or Spook while the multiplayer round continues. Ghosts can use their anytime cards; only Reset revives them. Healing caps at starting hearts +2. At 25% safe cells remaining, the Danger zone adds 1 to unblocked mine damage.
